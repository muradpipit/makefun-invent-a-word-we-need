**youtoo** (n.): the automatic "you too" you say to the waiter who just told you to enjoy your meal.

One invented English word and its one-line definition for make.fun's "Invent a Word We Need" bounty ($5 in USDC).

## Why this word

English has no word for the automatic wrong reply everyone has given at least once: the waiter says "enjoy your meal", and before your brain catches up, your mouth has already said "you too". *Youtoo* names that little social misfire. It is short, sayable and spellable, and it sounds like English could have grown it — the way "goodbye" grew out of "God be with ye".

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

The program writes the word and its definition to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output/entry.txt`). The output is a fixed 96-character line — inside the bounty's 1–140 character gate — and running it twice in a clean environment produces byte-identical files.
